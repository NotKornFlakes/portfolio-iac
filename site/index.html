About me

I'm Tim, a Help Desk Analyst at Upstate Medical University. I support hospital and office staff by phone and ticket: Active Directory and Microsoft 365 accounts and access, set up under least privilege, and the everyday problems that stop people working. I work my own queue to service-level targets and escalate with clear handoff notes.

Before IT, I ran my own home repair business for nearly five years. I was the whole company: I talked customers through what was wrong, scoped the work and owned each job until it was done. I still work that way: clear communication, an honest scope and following through.

I graduated from SUNY Oswego in December 2024 with a B.A. in Information Science, studying full time while running the business, and I hold the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) certification.
