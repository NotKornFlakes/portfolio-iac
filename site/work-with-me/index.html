How it works

  1. Send me an email with a sentence or two about the task that's eating your time.
  2. A short, free call to understand it. If an existing tool already does the job, I'll tell you.
  3. A written plan and quote before any work starts: what I'll build, what access it needs, and what you'll get.
  4. Build, test and hand over, with a short guide on how it works and what to do if it stops.
03

How I build

  • Secure by default. Every automation gets the least access it needs. Passwords and keys are never stored in plain text, and data isn't kept longer than the job requires.
  • Reliable. I test with real examples, and anything that runs on its own tells someone when it fails.
  • Yours to keep. Accounts and tools stay in your name, and everything is documented, so you're never locked into me.
  • Permission in writing. I never touch a system without written agreement on what's allowed.
  • No kickbacks. I don't earn commissions on tools I recommend. Free and open-source comes first.
  • Separate from my day job. This is independent work, done on my own time and equipment.

Starting small, on purpose. I'm taking on a few small projects at a time, and I'll be upfront about what's in my wheelhouse and what isn't. I'm not a 24/7 IT provider, a penetration-testing firm or a compliance auditor. If you need one of those, I'll help you find someone who can.
